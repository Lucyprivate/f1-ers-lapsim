# Open-source methods and research boundaries

This phase investigates structural error and control-learning implementation in parallel. The user has authorized training inside the declared model even when historical accuracy gates fail, provided physical and numerical checks pass and the registered FP2 test stays sealed. An eligible model experiment is therefore not a release of an accurate real-car strategy.

The public baseline is commit `043fc334d7913340c3524d185c3a0413ee98bd7d`. New research lives under `f1ers_v7.research` with separate schemas, configurations and results. The original scientific repository, frozen experiments and test-access ledger are preserved. Source revisions and runtime versions are recorded in [sources.json](../config/research/sources.json); the actual experiment freeze must also record its code, configuration, data and installed-dependency identities.

## Fixed experiment conditions

Primary learning keeps the declared 800 kg mass, 400 kW equivalent ICE wheel power, ideal DC-equivalent energy ledger and 2–4 MJ working window. Cold starts use exactly 2 MJ and the frozen control-line speed and FSM boundary. Warm starts come from one frozen qualified baseline carry, with the same complete boundary pool for both backends. Evaluations never optimize their initial speed, restore observed speed during a lap, scale whole-lap power, or replenish energy at a boundary.

The 36-feature research observation adds episode initial speed and initial DC power to the previous 34 features. This reduces terminal-boundary aliasing and uses a fresh checkpoint contract. It is not a proof of complete Markov observability: arbitrary initial FSM states can still differ in closure-relevant fields. The supported pool must constrain those fields explicitly, or expose additional state. Current state, initial boundary, masked raw action, executed force/power and termination reason remain auditable.

The two parallel tracks share an immutable learning baseline. A structural physics candidate cannot be substituted into an ongoing learner. Adopting one requires a new source/configuration freeze, teacher, affected training and numerical/physical qualification. The separate physics comparison may refit the five previously authorized equivalent parameters: friction, drag area, downforce area, replay equivalent power and the single replay brake gain. It retains the original bounds, a seed-2026 64-point Sobol screen, three distinct complete physically feasible starts, and at most 300 iterations and 600 evaluations per start. Fewer than three viable starts withholds fitting. New axle shares, load-transfer geometry and battery/auxiliary losses are declared fixed sensitivities, never freely fitted. Per-corner grip tuning and speed-residual route alignment remain prohibited.

## Physics hypotheses and counterevidence

| Hypothesis | Expected observation | Counterevidence or unresolved limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The historical route or control-line registration creates fictitious lateral demand. | Position uncertainty and curvature/boundary diagnostics explain specific infeasible locations across calibration laps. | Better position error with failed route bounds is rejected. Previous position-derived route candidates failed bounds; no improvement is assumed. |
| A brake-switch mapping makes requested braking vanish near the lateral limit. | Request remains finite under the independent mapping, while the executor reports actual clipping by combined tire capacity. | Persistent failure with sufficient requested braking points to geometry, incoming speed or capability instead. |
| Whole-vehicle force budgets overstate rear traction or regeneration. | Rear axle constraints restrict ICE plus K traction, or regenerative share of a fixed negative wheel request. | Rear static/aero shares, load transfer and lateral allocation are unmeasured; this is a structural sensitivity, not identified vehicle physics. |
| Small energy losses overturn ideal sustainable-lap eligibility. | Battery and auxiliary ledgers produce reproducible deficits or an early floor event. | A legal ideal-energy result does not establish real SOC feasibility. Cold-floor failures may preclude a complete sensitivity lap. |
| Learner defects, observation aliasing or raw-action entropy obscure a useful policy. | Matched sampled-update/Q-versus-return checks isolate a concrete implementation discrepancy or a repeatable qualified improvement. | Failure to improve, or improvement inside an inaccurate model only, does not establish real-car benefit. |

The axle nominal setting is rear static share 0.5, rear aero share 0.5 and height/wheelbase 0.1. The eight sensitivity corners are the Cartesian product of rear shares 0.45/0.55 and height/wheelbase 0.05/0.15, plus the separate nominal case. Rear longitudinal transfer must change sign correctly under braking. ICE plus K share rear positive traction; rear regeneration plus rear friction share rear negative force; front friction uses the front budget. A declared lateral split based on the static yaw-balance fraction is a causal modeling choice; an optimized feasible allocation is an oracle upper bound and must be labeled separately. Neither allocation identifies the real front/rear tire forces.

Loss sensitivity has nine cases from one-way battery efficiency 1.0/0.98/0.95 and auxiliary power 0/50/100 W. With signed DC power `P_DC` in kW, the declared ES balance is

`dE_MJ/dt = eta_battery * max(-P_DC, 0)/1000 - max(P_DC, 0)/(1000*eta_battery) - P_aux_W/1e6`.

The same efficiency applies to each direction, giving round-trip efficiency `eta_battery²`. Existing wheel/DC conversion efficiencies remain separate. FIA power and recharge counters stay on their declared DC side; battery charge/discharge, conversion losses and auxiliary work are separate ledger entries. Reserves and event checks must use actual ES costs. Intentional losses are not numerical balance residuals.

At a cold 2 MJ floor, positive auxiliary drain with no accepted regeneration causes an immediate physical failure. Locate and retain the actual event; do not clamp the ledger, lift the initial energy/floor, force regen under positive wheel demand, or report a fabricated complete lap. A saved warm-state sensitivity may be reported separately only if preregistered and must not replace the cold gate.

## Teacher and learner acceptance

The teacher searches 16 action blocks from three fixed action starts, with one optional 32-block refinement inside the same total cap of 600 attempted exact search evaluations. Initial speed, energy and FSM are fixed. “Shooting” means action-parameter search only. CasADi proposals use a conditional fixed-mode surrogate; changes in event timing can invalidate that schedule. Exact causal execution, independent proxy checks and numerical qualification decide feasibility. Infeasible proposals and prefix trajectories are retained. Mandatory certification calls must be counted separately if they are outside a clearly labeled search-only cap.

A nonconstant teacher is adopted only after a qualified improvement greater than 0.10 seconds against the baseline under identical fixed-cold six-carry conditions and the same preregistered time statistic. Otherwise the legal fallback remains eligible and the negative result is reported. Finite candidates do not prove global optimality.

The new SAC budget is 120 attempted episodes: two backends, seeds 2026/2027/2028, cold/warm conditions, and ten attempts per seed/condition/backend. A durable shared claim is written before reset. Interrupted, crashed and physically failed attempts consume budget; resumed branches and clones do not create replacement attempts. Report started, completed, failed and interrupted counts separately. A completed software episode can end in a physical failure and is not automatically a legal lap. Teacher, BC, diagnostics and mandatory deterministic evaluation rollouts have separate declared counters. DAgger is not reported without an expert that replans from actual learner states.

Both backends must use the same physical executor, feature order, action mapping, normalizer, reward convention, discount, return scale, temperature units, sampled batches, update and evaluation schedules for their claimed comparison. Braking actor actions are physical no-ops in this deployment-only family; any mask must agree across actor entropy/gradient, Bellman targets and soft Monte Carlo return diagnostics. Use the pre-transition wheel-demand sign. Masking the no-op does not enlarge the negative wheel-force or recharge budgets.

Physical failure and lap completion are terminal transitions with no critic bootstrap. Administrative truncation retains its unfinished prefix and is never a qualified lap. Best and last retained checkpoints are qualified under the same fixed-cold six-carry conditions; single-boundary warm/cold times cannot directly choose the final winner. Learned candidates also require improvement greater than 0.10 seconds before adoption, plus cold-first and carried-late numerical qualification. Smaller differences remain diagnostic and do not replace the incumbent. Record differences required by a custom PyTorch/SB3-aligned implementation rather than describe the algorithms as identical. Differential checks cover the tested batches, dtypes and tolerances only.

## Data access and scientific interpretation

Only Race laps 3–6 inform structural hypotheses, route checks, teacher search and the bounded five-parameter physics fit. Physics candidate parameters are frozen before Race laps 7/9 select among the three preregistered models; these laps cannot refit parameters, realign the route or expand the candidate family. Controller development scores are descriptive evaluations of frozen candidates and do not enter teacher search or learning selection. Laps 7/9 are repeatedly used historical development data and are not a new test. The historical 3–9 raw source may be read to construct a role-specific view, but calibration APIs return only calibration laps.

The existing Austrian FP2, driver 63, MEDIUM protocol and shared access identity stay sealed. Research commands must have no FP2 acquisition, adaptation, late-array loading or scoring path. A new branch, dependency environment or cloned public layout does not authorize a new test access or reset the original ledger.

Formal accuracy limits remain complete-lap average speed RMSE <=15 km/h, each lap's time error <=2%, and each sector's time error <=1 second. Sector 1/2/3 speed RMSE is diagnostic; there is no new sector-RMSE threshold. Partial trajectories receive no formal accuracy score. Check fixed-demand output resampling separately from internal integration refinement, with adjacent time differences <=0.10 seconds and speed differences <=0.5 km/h, plus physical checks.

Historical accuracy failed before this phase. Training authorization changes the research scope, not those thresholds. Reports must distinguish independent method implementation, synthetic regressions, actual dependency/backend execution, historical model scores and sealed new-session validation. Results describe the fastest eligible retained candidate in a finite declared model experiment. They do not claim complete FIA compliance, identified SOC, a global optimum, or a real-car lap-time benefit.
